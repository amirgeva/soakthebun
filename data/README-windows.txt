Soak the Bun
============

You're a little water drop, and your job is to push every hamburger bun
into a puddle. Buns can only be pushed, never pulled, and only one at a
time. All 999 levels are generated by the game and are always solvable.

To play, run soakthebun.exe. Keep the DLL files in the same folder.

Controls
--------
Arrow keys          Walk / push a bun (hold to keep walking)
U, Z or Backspace   Undo (hold to undo repeatedly)
R                   Restart the level
H                   Hint: show the best next push
M                   Mute / unmute
Esc                 Back to the menu
F11 or Alt+Enter    Toggle fullscreen

In the menu, Up/Down choose an item, Left/Right change the level
(Page Up/Page Down jump by 10), digits type a level number, and Enter
starts playing.

Your progress is saved in %APPDATA%\SoakTheBun\SoakTheBun\progress.ini
